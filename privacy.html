Privacy Policy

01

Acceptance of Terms

By registering on the nihilium Minecraft server, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not register.

By visiting this website, you agree to analytics-related terms of this privacy policy.

02

Data we collect

We only collect data that is essential for Nihilium

  • Player username
  • Player password
  • Player UUID
  • Player settings
  • Player homes

However, the software we use also stores data we do not need or use such as

  • Player IP
  • Player messages

This website uses umami.is to collect analytics data. See (1) WHAT INFORMATION DO WE COLLECT? in their privacy policy

03

Data usage

Providing service

We use the data we collect to provide features such as homes, configurable mace damage or a configurable player list that require us to store the above mentioned data.

Promoting our service

We use analytics data collected through our analytics platform to bring more players to the server.

04

Data storage and security

Providing service

We store the data we collect securely. We will make sure unauthorized third parties do not have access to this data. For information about the security of data collected on this website see the umami privacy policy

05

Data sharing

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties.

06

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. It is up to the player to check when it gets updated. This website is open source on github where the edit history is present and you can get notified when there are changes.

For questions or concerns, contact us via the information present on the about page.
