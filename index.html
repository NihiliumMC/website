How to join

Ask us on Discord
How do I join on Java Edition?+

Open Minecraft Java Edition, choose Multiplayer → Add Server, and enter as the server address. Save it, select Nihilium, and click Join Server. Follow the in-game instructions to register or sign in.

Can I play on Bedrock Edition?+

Yes. On mobile or Windows, open Play → Servers → Add Server. Use bedrock.nihilium.org as the address and 25406 as the port. Console editions may require an additional method to connect to a custom server.

What does anarchy mean here?+

You choose how to play, and you are responsible for your actions. Deliberately lagging the server, exploiting vulnerabilities, or interfering with its operation can lead to restrictions. Read the Terms of Service for the details.
